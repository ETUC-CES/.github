### 🇪🇺 Who we are
Founded in 1973 and based in Brussels, the European Trade Union Confederation
represents **45 million workers** through **94 national trade union confederations**
in **42 European countries**, plus **10 European Trade Union Federations**.

We speak with a single voice for working people in the European institutions
and in European social dialogue.

### 💻 About this organisation
This GitHub organisation hosts the digital and technical work of ETUC. Our repositories are private and used internally.
